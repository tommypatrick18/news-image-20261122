# The World in One Day — Sunday 22 November 2026

One image that sums up the world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026: 42 verified
stories — 4 lead stories, 12 more in cards, 26 in brief, a strip of the day's five
biggest numbers, and a map of where it all happened.

- **Lead stories:** COP31 nears a final deal in Antalya · Iran strikes Bahrain and
  tankers in Hormuz · Russian missile kills 2 in Kryvyi Rih · an AI-assisted claim
  of a Riemann proof passes a Lean check (shown as a claim — the Clay Institute
  still lists it unsolved).
- **Also:** Wall Street's worst week since COVID, Nvidia −14% on an SEC probe,
  Bitcoin back above $60,000, BepiColombo reaches Mercury, Verstappen wins a chaotic
  Las Vegas GP, the FBI probes a hack of its national crime database.
- **Date check:** every story happened on, or was first reported on, 22 November
  2026. Market figures are Friday 20 November closes, first reported 21–22 November
  (markets were closed on Sunday). Claims are shown as claims.

## The entry

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. The image is 1024×1024, 309.1 KiB — inside the gate
(≤1 MiB, ≤1024×1024). Two runs write the same bytes.

- `entry.png` — the image (SHA-256 `a964e0a6c95fffc34792bdae42e625044a048872a4cd0a73daf80c0434086858`)
- `SOURCES.md` — all 42 stories with their primary source, URL and publication date
- `SPEC.md` — layout, typography, map and build notes

## How it was built

The image was composed fresh for 22 November 2026: research from primary sources
(AP, Reuters, BBC, AFP, Nature, Deadline and others), verified story by story, then
laid out as a newspaper front page (masthead with the big date, lead stories, cards,
figure strip, world map with numbered pins, index) and rendered to PNG. No entry
from any other day was copied or imitated.
